
# 🚕 Urban Mobility Analysis: Strategy and Weather Impact in Chicago

## 1. Project Objective

Analyze the taxi transportation market in Chicago by integrating data extracted through SQL and processed using Python.

The study aims to identify the leading taxi companies by trip volume, map the main urban destinations, and test the hypothesis that weather conditions affect trip duration between downtown Chicago (the Loop) and O'Hare International Airport.

## 2. Results

- **Market Leaders Identification:** Identified the taxi companies with the largest market share based on trip volume.
- **Destination Hotspots:** Identified the top 10 Chicago neighborhoods with the highest frequency of drop-offs, providing insights to support fleet optimization.
- **Statistical Validation of Weather Impact:** Evaluated whether the average duration of airport trips changes significantly on rainy Saturdays, supporting more accurate travel time estimates for users.
- **Data Consolidation:** Delivered an analytical report combining operational metrics with external variables, including weather conditions.

## 3. Tools & Technologies

- **Programming Language:** Python
- **Libraries:** Pandas (data processing), Matplotlib and Seaborn (data visualization), SciPy.stats (statistical testing).
- **Data Extraction:** SQL (advanced queries to generate the analysis dataset).
- **Development Environment:** VS Code.

## 4. Skills Developed

- **SQL-Python Integration:** Performed the final data processing stage using datasets consolidated through complex SQL queries.
- **Frequency and Distribution Analysis:** Created visualizations to identify the top 10 destinations, facilitating the interpretation of traffic volumes.
- **Time-Based Data Processing:** Converted and manipulated datetime objects to filter trips specifically for Saturdays.
- **Hypothesis Testing:** Formulated null (\(H_0\)) and alternative (\(H_1\)) hypotheses and applied the Student's t-test for independent samples to evaluate differences in average trip duration under different weather conditions.

## 5. Future Improvements

- **Seasonality Analysis:** Expand the dataset to include Chicago's severe winter months and evaluate the impact of snowfall on urban mobility.
- **Geospatial Visualization:** Implement heatmaps using libraries such as Folium or GeoPandas to visualize the fastest routes.
- **Fare Prediction:** Develop regression models to estimate trip costs based on average duration and traffic conditions.
